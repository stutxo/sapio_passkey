      About this wallet

      Test coins only, not production custody. Keep this exact origin. Losing your original passkey or the pinned signer has no recovery path.

      This is an experimental test-coin wallet, not a production custody assurance. This page, its hosting and its WASM assets must be trusted to display and authorize the right payment. Authenticator attestation is not requested. Private passkey material stays with your authenticator.

      The gateway stores your public passkey credential ID and public key before a new wallet is ready to receive. On login, one fresh passkey approval is verified locally against that registered public key; no transaction is signed. This public registry is not a spending credential or verified authenticator attestation. Registration is immutable, and the gateway must remain available to look up your identity when logging in. Losing your original passkey still loses access.

      This browser remembers one passkey account using public credential metadata in IndexedDB, never private keys. Returning visits and logout keep that account selected; a cancelled or failed login never creates a replacement wallet. First-time setup is unavailable once an account is known. Browsers cannot silently enumerate passkeys on another device: select your existing passkey there rather than setting up another. BDK history and signed transaction bytes are also cached locally and may be read by anyone with access to browser storage. Clearing browser data removes the remembered selection and local signed bytes, not the registered account. If registration is incomplete, keep the original setup tab open and retry with the same passkey; no receive wallet is ready until registration succeeds.

      Signer identity not loaded

      Both your original passkey and the configured oracle are needed to spend. The operator must separately verify the Nitro identity, attestation and exact policy digest before pinning them. This page does not check TEE attestation and never automatically trusts a newly fetched signer identity. Local regtest software signers have no enclave assurance and their identities are ephemeral.

      This wallet reuses one address for receiving and change, making your activity linkable. The configured gateway (not loaded) stores public passkey identities and sees lookup, registration and transaction requests; the chain indexer sees chain requests and transactions. Public registry records can be retrieved by credential ID. The indexer is trusted for availability, completeness, spentness and chain anchors. BDK tracks transaction history, balances and replacements; the wallet verifies funding transaction IDs and outputs, policy authorizations, signatures and exact finalization. This is not SPV or full-node validation. The indexer can omit transactions or report stale chain information, and reorganizations can change confirmations.

      Your exact origin and hostname are part of the policy. Keep using this same origin and your registered discoverable ES256 passkey. Login reads the registered public identity and verifies one fresh, context-bound approval before opening the wallet and reading its context-bound history cache. A new payment requires a separate review and fresh transaction approvals. Neither factor is replaced by the public registry or browser cache. The spending policy has no assertion expiry or replay counter, even though this page uses fresh signing challenges. Web Locks prevent this origin's tabs from concurrently opening the same wallet; they cannot coordinate other devices. If the cache is lost, reopening reconstructs chain-visible history but cannot recover signed bytes that never reached the network. Browser storage is not a backup.
