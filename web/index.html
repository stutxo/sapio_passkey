      Trust and privacy limits — read before funding

      This is an experimental test-coin wallet, not a production custody assurance. This page, its hosting and its WASM assets must be trusted to display and authorize the right payment. Authenticator attestation is not requested. Private passkey material stays with your authenticator. Public wallet details remain in this tab unless you explicitly enable public-history caching.

      Both your original passkey and the configured oracle are needed to spend. The operator must separately verify the Nitro identity, attestation and exact policy digest before pinning them. This page does not check TEE attestation and never automatically trusts a newly fetched signer identity. Local regtest software signers have no enclave assurance.

      This wallet reuses one address for receiving and change, making your activity linkable. The configured gateway (not loaded) and chain indexer see your requests and transactions. The indexer is trusted for availability, completeness, spentness and chain anchors. BDK tracks transaction history, balances and replacements; the wallet verifies funding transaction IDs and outputs, policy authorizations, signatures and exact finalization. This is not SPV or full-node validation. The indexer can omit transactions or report stale chain information, and reorganizations can change confirmations.

      Your exact origin and hostname are part of the policy. Keep using this same origin and discoverable passkey. Opening an existing wallet reconstructs public details with two no-spend approvals before any optional history cache is read. This does not replace either factor or provide recovery. The policy has no assertion expiry or replay counter, even though this page uses fresh signing challenges. Web Locks prevent this origin's tabs from concurrently opening the same wallet; they cannot coordinate other devices. Without a public cache, reopening reconstructs chain-visible history but cannot recover signed bytes that never reached the network.
